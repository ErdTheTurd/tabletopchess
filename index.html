Tabletop Chess

A chessboard that sits on the table in front of you.

On Apple Vision Pro, the board anchors to any real table in your room. Look at a piece and pinch to pick it up. Play a built-in computer opponent, or pass the game to a friend.

What it does

A short list of what is planned. Nothing more.

  • On a real table

    The 3D board anchors to a table in your room, so the set sits where a physical board would.

  • Look and pinch

    Look at a piece and pinch to pick it up. Legal moves highlight before you place it.

  • A computer opponent

    Play a built-in opponent at a few difficulty levels. The game runs on the device.

  • Pass-and-play

    Play a friend on the same Vision Pro, taking turns at the same board.

  • The usual rules

    Castling, en passant, promotion, check, checkmate, and stalemate. Undo a move, or start a new game.

  • Works offline

    No account and no internet connection. You can play with the device on its own.
